Privacy Policy

WCO Suite

Last updated September 25, 2026

WCO Suite is a browser extension that replaces and enhances the video player on WCO-family cartoon and anime streaming sites (wco.tv, wcostream.tv, wcoflix.tv, and related mirrors). This page explains, plainly, what the extension does and does not do with your information.

In short: WCO Suite does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with us or with anyone else. Every setting it remembers stays on your own device, in your browser's local extension storage, and is never sent anywhere.

What the extension does

WCO Suite injects a custom video player, pop-out player window, and playback controls into pages on the WCO sites listed in its permissions. It reads information already present on those pages (such as the video source and episode title) so it can build its player and offer features like autoplay, quality selection, and audio/caption track switching. It does not read or interact with any other website.

Information stored locally

The extension uses the browser's storage API to remember your preferences on your own device only, so they carry over between episodes and shows. This includes:

CategoryExamples
Playback preferencesVolume, playback speed, autoplay mode, next-episode countdown timing
Track preferencesPreferred audio language, caption/subtitle language, caption appearance (color, size, font)
Player preferencesFullscreen behavior, pop-out window settings, whether onboarding hints have been dismissed

None of this data leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, to the WCO sites, or to any third party. We do not operate a server that the extension talks to, and the extension has no analytics or tracking code.

Information we do not collect

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • No browsing history or activity outside the specific WCO sites the extension is built for
  • No authentication credentials, payment information, or health information
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind

Permissions the extension requests

PermissionWhy it's needed
storageSaves your playback and settings preferences locally on your device, as described above.
tabsLets the pop-out player window communicate with the site tab it was opened from (to stay in sync during playback), detects when that tab closes, and navigates to the next/previous episode on supported sites.
Host permission (WCO site domains)Lets the extension inject its custom player and controls on the specific WCO streaming domains it's built for. It has no access to any other website.

Third parties

WCO Suite does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection service. The only network requests the extension makes are the ordinary requests your browser already makes to load video content from the WCO sites you're visiting — the extension does not add any requests of its own to any other destination.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the extension after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
